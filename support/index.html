Support

Answers to the questions people ask most. If yours is not here, write to us.

How do I play?

Tap Play and say the phrase on the screen, or anything you like. Esrever plays it backwards. Listen a few times, tap Copy it and copy the backwards sound. Esrever then flips your copy forwards, so you hear how close you got.

My copy does not sound like the phrase

Copy the sound you hear, not the words you said: it is meant to sound like nonsense. Short phrases are easiest. Listen to the backwards sound a few times before you record, and try again as often as you like.

The microphone does not work

Esrever needs the microphone to record. If you turned it off, open the Settings app, tap Apps, then Esrever, and turn on Microphone. A phone call or another app that is recording can also hold the microphone; end it and try again.

I cannot hear anything

Turn up the volume with the buttons on the side of your device. Esrever plays sound even when the silent switch is on. If headphones or a speaker are connected, the sound goes there.

How does party mode work?

Two to eight players pass one phone. One player says a secret phrase, the next hears it backwards and copies it, and everyone hears the copy flipped and guesses what was said. Right guesses score points. Your first party game is free; later ones are part of Esrever Pro.

Where are my recordings?

Only on your device, in My clips. Your newest clips stay there (5, or 30 with Pro), and with Pro you can keep any clip for good. Deleting the app deletes your recordings. Share a clip to keep a copy elsewhere.

How do I change the language?

Esrever follows your device's language. To choose another, open Settings in the app and tap a language under Language.

What is in Esrever Pro?

Every party game after your first, every phrase pack, voice effects (chipmunk, giant, robot and echo) and playback speed, and keeping clips for good. The game itself, with unlimited tries and no ads, is free.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Esrever and tap Cancel Subscription. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in and choose the purchase.
