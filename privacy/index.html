Privacy policy

Effective 21 November 2026

In short

Esrever is made by Dillon Pinto. It has no account, no advertising and no tracking. Your recordings stay on your device. This policy explains the few cases where information leaves your device.

The microphone and your recordings

Esrever uses the microphone only while you are recording, after you allow it. Recordings are turned around, and changed by voice effects, on your device. They are stored only in the app on your device, together with any phrase you type, and we cannot hear or see them. Deleting a clip, or the app, removes them. A recording leaves your device only if you share it, and then it goes where you choose to send it.

Party mode

The names you enter for players are stored only on your device, to fill in the next party.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. To confirm what you have bought and to restore it on your devices, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc., which receives an anonymous identifier created by the app, your purchase records (what was bought, when, and whether it renewed or was refunded) and basic technical information such as your country, device type and app version. It does not receive your name, email address or recordings. RevenueCat's privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

What we do not do

No advertising, no analytics kits, no tracking across other apps or websites, and no selling or sharing of personal information.

Reports from Apple

Apple may give us totals, such as how many people installed the app or how often it stopped working, if you have agreed to share analytics with app developers. These reports do not identify you.

Children

Esrever does not ask anyone, of any age, for personal information. Recordings and names stay on the device, and purchases go through Apple.

Your rights

Because we hold no account or profile for you, there is usually nothing for us to look up, correct or delete. If you write to us, we will have your email address and will use it only to reply. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal information, and to complain to your data protection authority. To use any of these rights, including for the anonymous purchase identifier held by RevenueCat, email us.

Where information is handled

RevenueCat is based in the United States. Information sent to it is handled under its own policy and the safeguards it describes.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
